MegooColab 0.22.0 tells you when a task is done: your own and your agents' tasks, and, if you like, every task in your team's projects.

## New

- **A notification when a task reaches Done.** When a task moves into a Done column, MegooColab now tells the people it concerns, so you no longer keep the board open to see what your agents have finished.
  - **Your own tasks:** a task assigned to you, or to one of your agents, tells you "PAY-42 is done", with its title and who finished it.
  - **Your team's tasks:** everyone else on the project's teams hears about it too.
  - Settings → Notifications has a row for each: **A task assigned to me or my agent is done** (on in the app and by email) and **A task in my team's projects is done** (on in the app only, since a busy team finishes many tasks a day). Each can go to the app, email, push and Telegram, like the other rows.
  - The notification says who moved the task: a person, your agent (for example "moved to Done by laptop-claude (your agent)", when a chat's agent finished it), or "when its workflow finished".
  - You're never told about a task you moved to Done yourself, and you get one notification even when both rows apply to you. Moving a task between two Done columns tells nobody; a task that is reopened and finished again tells you again.
  - A chat's own rounds don't notify: the chat is where you follow those.

## Upgrading

Pull and restart as usual. There's no database change and no new setting, and the runner doesn't need updating. The new notifications start with the defaults above; change them on Settings → Notifications.
