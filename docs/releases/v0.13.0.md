MegooColab 0.13.0 lets you **expand a chat over the whole window** on a desktop, so a long conversation with an agent is easier to read and follow.

## New

### Expand a chat
- **The button:** ⤢ **Expand chat** sits in an open chat's header, beside **End chat**, on the Chats page and in a project's **Chats** tab.
- **Expanded,** the chat covers the whole browser window: the menu, the top bar and the chat list make way. The header stays at the top, with the title, the project, the agent, the state and the steps.
- **Readable on a big screen:** the conversation and the message box sit in a centered column about 900 pixels wide, so lines don't stretch across a wide monitor.
- **Everything works as before:** "Following live", the ↑, ↓ and "waiting on you" buttons, images, dialogs and menus.
- **To put it back,** press ⤡ **Collapse chat**, or **Esc**. If a dialog, a menu or a list of suggestions is open, Esc closes that first.
- **Remembered in this browser:** once you expand a chat, chats open expanded until you collapse one.
- **Desktop only:** the button shows on screens at least 768 pixels wide. On a phone a chat already fills the screen.

## Upgrading

Pull and restart as usual. Nothing else changes, and runner 0.8.0 stays current.
