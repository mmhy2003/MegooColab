MegooColab 0.27.1 stops the flood of "done" notifications from plan steps, and makes the Dashboard's Chats card easier to read on a phone.

## Improved

- **The Dashboard's Chats card shows each chat on two lines.** The first line shows the project key, then the chat's title, and opens the chat. The second line shows the status, in its colour. Before, the title and the status shared one line: on a phone they cut each other short and the project key was usually hidden. A long title now ends with "…", and a long status wraps instead of being cut.

## Fixed

- **Subtasks no longer send "done" notifications.** Each plan step is a subtask that the workflow moves to Done during Build, and each one told the whole team "KEY is done". A task with 8 steps sent 9 notifications. Now a subtask reaching Done notifies nobody, whoever moved it. Stories, tasks, bugs and epics notify as before.

## Upgrading

Pull and restart as usual. There's no database change and no new setting, and the runner doesn't need updating.
