MegooColab 0.14.2 lets you expand a task's own page over the whole window, as you already can with a chat.

## New

- **Expand a task's page.** On a desktop, a task's own page has an Expand button (⤢) in its header, next to Delete. It works as it does in a chat:
  - **Over the whole window:** the task covers the menu and the top bar, so a long design, plan or conversation has room.
  - **Readable, not stretched:** the task and its details column stay side by side in a centred area up to 1280 px wide; on a smaller screen it simply uses the full width.
  - **Remembered:** your browser keeps the choice, so the next task you open is already expanded. Tasks and chats remember it separately.
  - **Esc collapses it,** or the Collapse button (⤡). Esc is left alone while you're typing in the title, the description or a comment, and while a menu, a picker or a dialog is open, so it closes that instead.
  - **Unchanged:** the task drawer on the board keeps its "Open full page" button, and on a phone, where a task already fills the screen, there's no button.

## Upgrading

Pull and restart as usual. Nothing else changes, and runner 0.8.0 stays current.
