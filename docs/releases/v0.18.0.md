MegooColab 0.18.0 lets a freestyle chat's agent manage the board for you (tasks, epics and sprints), and keeps the conversation in view while you write a Knowledge Base document with an agent.

## New

- **A freestyle chat's agent works on the board.**
  - **Tasks and epics:** it can list, read, create, change, move and delete them. Changes cover the title, description, type, priority and story points. It can also put a task under an epic, or take it out.
  - **Sprints:** it can plan, change, start, complete and delete sprints, and add tasks to them or take tasks out.
  - **You stay in charge.** It acts when you ask. When the idea is its own, it says what it would change and waits for your yes. It deletes only what you named or agreed to.
  - **It never assigns anyone,** so a chat can't start an agent's work by itself.
  - **It works only on the chat's own project, with the rights of the agent's owner.** Managing sprints still needs a project manager. Deleting a task still needs its reporter or a manager.
  - **Every change shows in the chat** as a line with links, for example "created the sprint Sprint 12 (16 – 27 Nov)", "added PAY-14 to Sprint 12" or "deleted ~~PAY-9~~". The changed task's own history also says "from the chat …", with a link back to the conversation.
  - **For people connecting their own agent over MCP,** the same tools work with a personal token:
    - `delete_task`, `list_sprints`, `create_sprint`, `update_sprint`, `start_sprint`, `complete_sprint`, `delete_sprint` and `set_task_sprint`;
    - `type` and `epic_key` on `update_task`;
    - `type` on `list_tasks`, so `type="epic"` lists the epics.

## Improved

- **The conversation stays in view while you write a document with an agent.**
  - **On a wide screen,** the conversation panel next to a long draft stays under the top bar as you scroll the draft. Its messages scroll inside the panel and open at the newest one, and the request box stays at the bottom of the panel.
  - **On a phone,** the page still uses the Draft and Conversation tabs.

## Upgrading

Pull and restart as usual. There's no database change and no new setting. The agent's instructions for freestyle chats update on their own. Runner 0.8.0 stays current.
