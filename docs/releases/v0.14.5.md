MegooColab 0.14.5 lets you pick several projects on the Chats page from a list you can search.

## Improved

- **Pick several projects on the Chats page, and search for them.** The Project dropdown is now a picker:
  - **Closed,** it shows the projects you picked as tags, each with its own ✕ to take it out (two of them, then "+N more"), or "All projects" when none is picked.
  - **Open,** a search box at the top matches a project's key or name and highlights the match; "No project matches …" says when nothing does.
  - **One checkbox per project,** with its key, its name and its chats: how many are active (in cyan), then how many in all. The projects picked when you opened it stay at the top, so a row doesn't jump away as you tick it.
  - **Select all** (or **Select shown** while you search) and **Clear** are one click away, with how many are picked beside them.
  - **From the keyboard:** ↑ and ↓ move through the list, Enter ticks, Esc closes.
  - **The state chips and their counts follow the projects you pick,** and the list drops a project's key from each row when only one is picked. Your browser remembers the picks; a project you can no longer see is left out.
  - A project's own Chats tab is unchanged: it shows that project's chats, with no picker.

## Upgrading

Pull and restart as usual. Nothing else changes, and runner 0.8.0 stays current.
