MegooColab 0.12.1 stops the **● Following live** note from covering what you're reading in a chat or a task's Activity.

## Fixed

- **"Following live" no longer hides the newest lines.** The note sits in the bottom-left corner while you follow a chat or a task's Activity. That's exactly where the newest lines are, so it covered them: the agent's latest message, or the last comment above the comment box. Chats and Activity now leave a small blank strip under their newest line, and the note sits in that strip. The strip stays when you scroll up, so the page doesn't jump when following starts or stops. This holds on desktops and on phones.

## Upgrading

Pull and restart as usual. Nothing else changes, and runner 0.8.0 stays current.
