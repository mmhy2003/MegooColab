MegooColab 0.13.2 makes **End chat** always ask before it ends a chat, so a stray click can't close a conversation for good.

## Fixed

- **End chat always asks first.** Ending a chat can't be undone: the chat becomes read-only, and to keep going you start a new one. Until now, End chat only asked while the agent was working. At any other time, such as while it waited for your reply, while a round was in review, or after a round had finished, one click ended the chat straight away. Now every End chat shows **End this chat?**, from the header button and from the ⋯ menu on a phone.
  - **While the agent is working,** it says, as before: "Stop the agent and end this chat? Whatever it committed stays on its branch."
  - **At any other time,** it says: "It becomes read-only and can't be reopened. To keep going, start a new chat."
  - **Cancel,** Esc or a click outside the question leaves the chat open. What ending does is unchanged.

## Upgrading

Pull and restart as usual. Nothing else changes, and runner 0.8.0 stays current.
