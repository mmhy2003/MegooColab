MegooColab 0.23.0 lets you hear new notifications, makes the task drawer as wide as you like, and keeps the selected result in view in Search.

## New

- **A chime when a new notification arrives.** While MegooColab is open in a browser, a short two-tone chime plays whenever the bell gets a new notification, so you notice it without watching the bell.
  - It plays whether the tab is in front or in the background. With several MegooColab tabs open, only one of them chimes, and notifications that arrive within 2 seconds of a chime don't chime again (the bell still counts them).
  - Browsers allow sound only after you have clicked or typed in a tab, so a tab you just reloaded stays silent until then.
  - Settings → Notifications has a new **Sound** card: **Play sound** to hear it, and **Turn off** or **Turn on**. The choice is kept per browser, and the sound is on until you turn it off.
- **Drag the task drawer's edge to resize it.** On a desktop, the task drawer that opens from the board or the backlog has a grip on its left edge. Drag it to make the drawer wider or narrower, from 720 px up to nearly the whole window.
  - The width is kept in your browser and used the next time a drawer opens, on the board and the backlog alike.
  - Double-click the edge to go back to the usual width. From the keyboard, focus the edge and use the arrow keys, Home and End.
  - On a narrow screen the drawer stays full width, as before.

## Improved

- **Search's group headings stay pinned, and the selected result stands out.** In the Search popup (Ctrl+K), the heading of each group (Tasks, Documents, …) stays at the top while the list scrolls, and the selected result has a cyan bar and tint.

## Fixed

- **The arrow keys keep the selected result in view in Search.** Before, pressing ↓ past the bottom of the list selected results you could no longer see. The list now scrolls just enough to show the selected result, and on the last one the "See all results" link too. A new search starts again at the top.
  - A mouse resting over the list no longer takes the selection back while the list scrolls under it; moving the mouse still selects a row.
  - Screen readers now hear which result is selected.

## Upgrading

Pull and restart as usual. There's no database change and no new setting. Runner 0.10.0 comes out at the same time and is worth updating to, mostly on Windows (see its note), but MegooColab 0.23.0 works with older runners too.
