MegooColab 0.5.0 adds passkeys as a second sign-in step, remembers the accounts you sign in with on each browser, and gives admins a clearer Users page where they can also delete someone or turn off their two-factor sign-in.

## New

### Passkeys
- Under **Profile › Two-factor sign-in**, a new **Passkeys** section lets you add your device's fingerprint, face, PIN or a security key, give it a name, and see when each one was added and last used.
- After the right password, an account with a passkey sees **Confirm it's you**: the browser's passkey prompt opens by itself. Someone who also has an authenticator app can choose **Use an authenticator code instead**.
- Two-factor sign-in is on while you have an authenticator app, a passkey, or both; the tab says which.
- Removing a passkey asks for your password and a passkey or your authenticator code, as turning off the app does.
- A passkey that can't be used says why: one that wasn't used, one the server couldn't check, or a page opened at another address than the server's own (passkeys need its HTTPS address).

### Remembered accounts on the sign-in page
- Each browser remembers the accounts signed in on it (the name and email only, never a password). The sign-in page lists them: pick one and type only its password. **Use another account** brings back the email and password form.
- **×** forgets an account on that browser. Signing out keeps it in the list, and **Forgot your password?** opens with its email filled in.

### Turning off someone's two-factor sign-in
- For a person who lost their phone or security key: an admin turns off their two-factor sign-in on **Admin › Users**. Their authenticator app and passkeys are removed, they sign in with their password, and they're told by email when email is set up.
- If the locked-out person is the only admin, run on the server: `docker compose exec backend python -m app.cli reset-two-factor <their email>`.

### Deleting someone
- **Admin › Users › ⋯ › Delete…** removes a person for good (anyone but yourself and the last admin), after you type their email.
- What is theirs goes with them: their account, access tokens, agents, notifications, team places and personal memory. The team's work stays: tasks (unassigned if they had them), comments, documents, decisions and Knowledge Base pages, shown as "Someone". Agent runs they started stay in the task's history.
- To only stop someone signing in, deactivating is still there and can be undone.

## Improved
- **Admin › Users** shows each person's two-factor sign-in (on, and how: app, passkeys) and last sign-in, has a search and filters (Active, Invited, Deactivated, Two-factor off), and puts every action for a person under one **⋯** menu, with only the ones that apply. On a phone each person is a card.

## Fixed
- On a task's own page, the corner buttons (back to the top, to the latest Activity and follow, waiting on you) now show and work, as they do in the side panel: the task scrolls inside its card instead of the whole page.

## Upgrading

```bash
docker compose pull
docker compose up -d
```

- Passkeys work only on the host name in `PUBLIC_URL`, over HTTPS (or on `localhost`). If you ever move MegooColab to another host name, existing passkeys stop working; people with an authenticator app use their code, and an admin turns off two-factor sign-in for the others (see `docs/install.md`, sections 6 and 14).
- Database changes apply by themselves when the backend starts. As always, take a backup first (`docs/install.md`, section 13).
- No runner update is needed: runner 0.2.0 is current.
