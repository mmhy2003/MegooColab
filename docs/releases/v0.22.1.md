MegooColab 0.22.1 fixes dragging a card into another column on the board when the columns are long.

## Fixed

- **A card dropped into another column lands there, wherever you let go of it.** Before, a card dragged out of a long column only stayed in the new column if you let go near that column's middle; anywhere else it went back to where it came from. On a long board, that middle could be far below the screen.
  - The card now goes into the column under the pointer: at the card you let go over, next to the nearer card between two, or at the end below the column's last card or in an empty column. The column's header takes a card too.
  - Moving cards with the keyboard works as before.

## Upgrading

Pull and restart as usual. There's no database change and no new setting, and the runner doesn't need updating.
