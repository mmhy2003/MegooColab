MegooColab 0.9.1 makes **Chats** easier to follow: the page stays put while a conversation scrolls, with the same corner buttons a task has, and a long chat title no longer jumbles its header. It also fixes the collapsed sidebar cutting off its icons and counts.

## Improved

### Chats stay in view
- The Chats page fills the window and stays put. The chat list scrolls on its own, under the project filter. In a chat, the header stays at the top and the message box at the bottom; only the conversation scrolls between them. The same goes for a project's **Chats** tab and on a phone.
- A chat opens at its newest message and follows what arrives, with a small **● Following live** note. Scroll up to read and it stops, so it never pulls you away; reach the bottom again and it follows again.
- The corner buttons from a task's page:
  - **↑** goes back to the top;
  - **↓** goes to the latest, with a count of the messages that arrived while you were reading;
  - **N waiting on you** goes to a question to answer, or a design or plan to approve, and highlights it. Each click goes to the next one.
- **The chat's header is tidier.** A long title gets its own lines, cut after two, and hovering it shows the whole title. The project, agent, state and steps go on the line under it, with **End chat** beside the title.
- When the chat waits on you, its state in the header ("waiting: answer the agent's question") is a button that takes you to it.

## Fixed

- **The collapsed sidebar no longer cuts off its icons and counts.** On a short screen, the menu's scrollbar took room the narrow sidebar didn't have. The closed sidebar now hides it; the mouse wheel still scrolls, and the scrollbar comes back as the sidebar opens.

## Upgrading

Pull and restart as usual. Nothing else changes, and runner 0.5.0 stays current.
